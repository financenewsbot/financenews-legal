FinanceNews Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FinanceNews handles information when users interact with the project and its integrations with third-party platforms.

2. Information processed

Depending on the features enabled, FinanceNews may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, profile information made available by connected platforms, and information required to create and publish content.

3. How information is used

Information is used to operate the service, connect authorized social media accounts, prepare content for publication, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security.

4. Third-party services

FinanceNews may integrate with services such as TikTok. Their processing of personal information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect credentials and other sensitive information. Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

6. Sharing of information

FinanceNews does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to connected platforms when necessary to perform a user-requested action, such as publishing a video.

7. User choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal information. They may also revoke platform authorization where supported.

8. Children's privacy

FinanceNews is not intended to be directed at children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service evolves. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the FinanceNews project owner through the GitHub repository.
